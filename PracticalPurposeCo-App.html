PPco. API

Practical Purpose Co.'s proposed single-operator integration for original product marketing on our own Pinterest business account, linked to our Etsy listings.

Current status: in development, not connected.

Our initial application (1632555) was denied. We are preparing a corrected disclosure; this page is not evidence of approved access or a working integration.

The intended workflow

  1. Use our own product details and original or licensed assets.
  2. Prepare three distinct local image/copy drafts per owned Etsy product—not Pinterest-native drafts.
  3. The owner selects each exact image, title, description, Etsy destination and board individually.
  4. After appropriate access approval and verified safeguards, use official OAuth and the API to create that selected Pin. No post-publication editing is planned.

Scope and data boundaries

Local agents and hosted AI may prepare original operator-supplied assets. Competitor research/benchmarking, scraping and third-party Pin transformation are excluded. Pinterest Trends research, analytics and retention remain on hold. Pinterest-returned data and derivatives must not be sent to hosted AI without permission. Credentials remain out of prompts/public code. The adapter, per-item approval and data controls still need implementation and verification before use.

Trial creations are creator-only test entities. Standard approval and an actual OAuth demonstration are needed before public publishing. This internal tool is not offered to customers or unrelated accounts.

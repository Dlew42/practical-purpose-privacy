The intended workflow

From our product to an owner-approved Pin.

These steps describe the intended integration, not a currently working connection.

  1. 01

    Start with our assets

    Use our product details and original or appropriately licensed artwork and photos for an owned Etsy listing.

  2. 02

    Prepare locally

    Create three distinct image and copy drafts in our own system. These are local drafts, not Pinterest-native drafts.

  3. 03

    Review each Pin

    The owner selects and approves the exact image, title, description, destination and board for each individual Pin.

  4. 04

    Publish when permitted

    With the appropriate access tier and an implemented connection, use official Pinterest OAuth and API access to create the approved Pin on our own account, linking to its Etsy listing.

Trial access is for creator-only testing; public publishing requires the appropriate production access. We do not plan post-publication edits through this tool.

Our boundaries

Original work and owner choice.

Local automation and hosted AI may help prepare operator-supplied original assets. Pinterest-returned data is not intended to be sent to hosted AI providers without permission.

Outside the initial scope

  • Competitor research or benchmarking
  • Scraping or transforming Pinterest-derived images
  • Pinterest Trends research, analytics and retention of Pinterest data pending permission and implementation

These features are excluded or on hold. The intended data boundaries and approval controls still need to be implemented and verified before use.

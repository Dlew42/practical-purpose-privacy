PPco. API · Practical Purpose Co.

Privacy policy

Effective November 5, 2026 · Integration not yet enabled

Operator: Practical Purpose Co.
Contact: Use Contact on our Etsy shop.

This notice is for our internal integration, not Etsy's corporate privacy policy or a substitute for a separate shop-customer notice.

1. Scope and status

PPco. API is a proposed internal, single-operator Pinterest integration for preparing original product marketing and creating individually approved Pins for our own business. It is not a public account-management service. Our application was rejected and is being corrected; there is no verified Pinterest connection or approved access at this time.

The practices below describe intended handling if the integration is authorized, implemented and enabled. They are not a claim that its controls have already been built. Registration alone does not collect Pinterest account data.

2. Information and purpose

Our own product details, original or appropriately licensed artwork, photos, draft copy and public Etsy listing links may be used to prepare three local image-and-copy drafts per owned product. The owner would select the exact content, destination and board before each Pin creation. Drafts would be kept in our system, not created as unpublished Pinterest-native drafts.

If authorized and needed for the enabled function, the integration may use the owner's Pinterest account identifiers, relevant board and own-Pin details, and OAuth authorization information to identify the account and create the selected Pin. It will not request Pinterest passwords or session cookies. It is not intended to collect buyer orders, delivery addresses, payment-card information, private Pinterest messages or unrelated users' account information.

Competitor research and benchmarking, scraping, Pinterest-derived image transformations and post-publication editing through the tool are outside the initial scope. Pinterest Trends research and analytics remain on hold pending permission and implementation.

3. Providers and sharing

Local automation and hosted AI or other providers may help prepare images and copy from operator-supplied product information and original or appropriately licensed creative assets, under those providers' terms. Pinterest-returned information and derivatives must not be sent to hosted AI providers without Pinterest's permission. Credentials must stay out of model prompts. Actual data flows must be checked before enabling the integration; these are intended boundaries, not verified safeguards.

We do not sell or rent Pinterest account information. If an approved Pin is created, its selected content is sent to Pinterest, where visibility depends on the applicable access tier and Pinterest's policies. Visitors following a listing link are subject to Etsy's practices. Information may also be disclosed where required by law. Pinterest, Etsy and the public-site host independently handle information arising from use of their services.

4. Storage, retention and security

The intended integration is local. Its planned credential handling is to use an authorized credential store, excluding tokens from public repositories, creative drafts, prompts and routine logs. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure. These controls require implementation and verification before connection.

Retaining Pinterest-returned data, including analytics or research results, is on hold pending permission and implementation. Necessary recovery identifiers and operation receipts require clarification; they are not assumed exempt. Our own original assets and locally authored drafts may be retained for review, reuse and recovery; this does not authorize retention of Pinterest-returned data.

5. Disconnection, deletion and rights

If a connection is implemented, the owner may revoke it through Pinterest's connected-app controls and remove locally held tokens. Revocation stops future access but does not automatically remove Pins already published; those can be managed on Pinterest.

For questions, access, correction or deletion requests about information handled by this integration, contact Practical Purpose Co. through the Etsy shop. We will verify requests as appropriate, respond as required by applicable law and delete information under our control unless retention is legally required. Requests about information independently held by Pinterest, Etsy or another service should also be directed to that service.

6. Children and updates

This internal business tool is not directed to children and is not intended to collect children's personal information. This notice must be reviewed and updated to reflect actual practices before the associated processing is enabled; material changes will be reflected in the public notice before new processing begins.

Related notices: Pinterest privacy policy, Etsy privacy policy and GitHub privacy statement.
